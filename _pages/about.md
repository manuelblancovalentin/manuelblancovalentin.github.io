A bit about me.

Small bio
======
I am a computer engineer working at the intersection of ASIC design, machine learning systems, and scientific instrumentation. My current work focuses on hardware-software co-designed ML accelerators, radiation-hardened digital systems for particle detectors, and neuromorphic learning architectures that can run under tight physical constraints.

I am completing a Ph.D. in Computer Engineering at Northwestern University, where I work on ASIC and ML accelerator research with collaborators at Fermilab, CERN, Columbia, and other institutions. Before that, I worked on deep learning for industrial imaging and reservoir characterization at Petrobras/CBPF, and on electronics, robotics, and instrumentation during my earlier training in Barcelona and Rio de Janeiro.

Across these projects, I like building the full stack: algorithms, software tools, RTL/HLS, physical implementation, verification, and the documentation needed for other people to use the work.
