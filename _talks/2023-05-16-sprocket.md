Technical report presented at [Fermilab](https://doi.org/10.2172/1896647), showcasing the design and characterization of a cryogenic readout integrated circuit (IC) with in-pixel analog-to-digital conversion (ADC) for Skipper CCD-in-CMOS sensors, achieving 100 kilo-samples per second (KSPS) per pixel. This work is part of the ongoing efforts to develop advanced readout electronics for low-noise, high-sensitivity imaging applications in particle physics and astrophysics.
