Talk given at the [FASTML 2025 Conference](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1496673/contributions/6637920/), presenting our research on ENABOL, a novel framework for enabling neural backpropagation on-chip learning in edge AI systems. This work focuses on the design and implementation of efficient algorithms and hardware architectures to facilitate real-time learning and adaptation in resource-constrained environments.

For more information about the research, please visit [the ENABOL project page](https://manuelblancovalentin.github.io/kappa_budgeting/).
